# Visual System

## Direction
Blue on black, with the feel of a kinetic typographic poster and an open research
index. The user chose an animated opening and quieter reading sections. Cadence's
text-reveal and page-transition examples inform the timing, not copied code.
The Awwwards animation collection informs the contrast in scale and composition.

## Rules
- Self-hosted Manrope; bright, spacious headings and restrained body typography.
- Black #080b12, electric blue #235af5, readable blue #79a8ff, text #f0f3fb.
- One signature motion: an original canvas field of interconnecting closed paths.
  It is decorative geometry, not a representation of research results.
- Native scrolling, text selection, links and details. No scroll hijacking,
  custom cursor, autoplay audio, loading gate or content-dependent animation.
- Desktop content uses a left heading rail and a wider right reading column;
  mobile becomes a single column. Reading pages prioritize a comfortable measure.
- Motion pauses offscreen, in background tabs, on request, and by default with
  reduced-motion preferences. All biography content works without JavaScript.
- Preserve every existing substantive statement and destination; do not add claims.
